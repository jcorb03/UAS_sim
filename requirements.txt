
UAS and simulation

SR-01
The system shall simulate the motion of a UAS in a two-dimensional horizontal environment.

SR-02
The UAS shall maintain a simulated position, velocity, heading and altitude.

SR-03
The UAS shall be capable of following a predefined sequence of mission waypoints.

Sensors and estimation

SR-04
The system shall simulate noisy GPS position measurements.

SR-05
The system shall simulate noisy IMU measurements of acceleration and angular rate.

SR-06
The system shall estimate the UAS position, velocity and heading using measurements from the simulated sensors.

SR-07
The system shall provide an estimate of state uncertainty alongside the estimated state.

Autonomy

SR-08
The UAS shall autonomously transition between mission states including waypoint navigation, surveillance, and return-to-base.

SR-09
The UAS shall detect a simulated object of interest using a simplified EO/IR-like sensor model based on range and field of view.

SR-10
Upon detecting an object of interest, the UAS shall autonomously alter its mission behaviour according to predefined decision logic.

Communications

SR-11
The system shall simulate communication between the UAS and a ground station, including configurable latency and packet loss.

SR-12
The UAS shall maintain autonomous mission execution during temporary loss of communication with the ground station.

Verification and experimentation

SR-13
The system shall record UAS truth state, sensor measurements, estimated state and mission events during simulation.

SR-14
The system shall calculate quantitative measures of navigation performance, including position estimation error.

SR-15
The system shall support repeatable experiments under different sensor and communication conditions.
